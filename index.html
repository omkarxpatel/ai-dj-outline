How the AI DJ listens

This DJ listens to your songs and learns each one. Switch on DJ mode and it runs the whole set. It chooses the tracks and their order, and shapes each song live with effects and enhancement.

    How it picks the next song
    BuiltWeights being tuned
    
    
    
      score =0.4 × key fit+0.4 × tempo fit+0.2 × energy fit
    
    
    
    
      
    Key fit
    Same slot on the Camelot wheel scores 1.0. Each step away costs about 0.2.
    
      
    Tempo fit
    Within 6% feels like the same tempo. Further apart, the score drops fast.
    
      
    Energy fit
    Similar loudness and brightness, so the next song doesn't jump out.
